Privacy policy

Hinge Arcade · effective 10 November 2026

Hinge Arcade has no accounts and no ads, and it never asks for your name, email or anything else about you. To learn which games people play most, for how long, and which levels are too hard, it counts anonymous play statistics with Google Analytics for Firebase (see below). Nothing is used to track you across other apps or websites, and the app never asks for or reads your device’s advertising identifier.

Anonymous play statistics

Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google, receives these statistics from the app:

  • That the app was opened for the first time, and how often and how long it is played
  • The app version, the device model, its iOS version and the language
  • Your general region, such as the country or city, which Google works out from a masked network address. This never uses your location (see Location below).
  • Which screens are opened (the gallery, a game’s levels, a game) and how long each game is played
  • When a level starts and how it ends: its stars, score and time, whether it was finished, left or restarted, and whether the fold was moved by the hinge or by the slider, tilt or drag

Each copy of the app is given a random identifier so the same player isn’t counted twice. It isn’t linked to you, your Apple Account or the advertising identifier. The app’s advertising features are switched off: it sends nothing for ads, ad personalization or ad measurement. Google keeps the detailed records for at most 14 months; after that only totals remain. Google’s own handling of the data is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Location

The Stargazer game can use your approximate location to show the stars above you. It asks first, and you can say no and still play: the game then shows a sky at 45°N. Your location is used only on your phone, at the moment the sky is drawn. It is never saved and never sent anywhere, including in the statistics above.

The hinge on iPhone Duo

On iPhone Duo the games read the angle of the phone’s hinge while you play. The angle is used only on your phone, at that moment. It is never saved and never sent anywhere.

Update check

When you open the app, it asks Apple’s App Store whether a newer version of Hinge Arcade is available, so it can offer you the update. The request names only the app and the App Store country to look in. Nothing about you or your phone is sent with it, and the app keeps only the date of the last check and any version you chose to skip. As with any request over the internet, Apple’s servers see the network address it comes from; Apple’s own privacy policy covers that.

Ratings

Now and then the app may show Apple’s own rating prompt. Any rating or review you give is handled by Apple, not by the app.

Game progress and settings

Your levels, scores and settings are saved only on your phone. Settings › Reset progress erases them, and deleting the app removes them completely.

Children

Hinge Arcade is made for a general audience. It asks no one, of any age, for their name, contact details or age, and the anonymous statistics above are the same for every player.

Third parties

The app includes two libraries that connect to the internet. Siren runs the update check described above, and its only request goes to Apple. Google Analytics for Firebase receives the anonymous statistics above. No other company receives anything from the app.
