Hinge Arcade

14 games you play by folding

Hinge Arcade turns the fold of iPhone Duo into 14 tiny games, each with its own world and 20 handmade levels. Open, close and hold the fold: it is the only controller you need. On other iPhones, an on-screen fold slider plays the same games.

No ads, no accounts, no purchases. Nothing leaves your phone.
