Common questions

Do I need an iPhone Duo?
The games are designed for its fold, but every game also plays on other iPhones. Use the angle slider, the ± buttons and the Fold button in the dock at the bottom of each game, or choose Tilt or Drag in Settings › Hinge to fold by tilting the phone or dragging the top half.
A level is too hard. Can I skip ahead?
Settings › Unlock every level opens all 280 levels.
Why does Stargazer ask for my location?
To draw the real stars above you. It uses an approximate location only on your phone and never sends it anywhere. If you say no, you still get a full sky to explore.
How do I start over?
Settings › Reset progress.
